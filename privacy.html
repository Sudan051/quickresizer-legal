Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 1, 2026

Photo Inhanser Pro is developed and maintained by Saurabh Kumar Sharma. This Privacy Policy explains how our application processes images, respects user privacy, and adheres to the highest data protection standards.

1. 100% On-Device AI Processing (Zero Server Upload)

Your privacy is our core architectural priority:

  • All photo editing, neural segmentation, AI object erasing, and color adjustments run strictly on your device using Apple Metal Shaders and Apple Vision Framework.
  • Your photos, edits, and camera captures are never uploaded, transmitted, or streamed to any remote cloud server or external third party.
  • What you create on your iPhone stays on your iPhone.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Photo Inhanser Pro does not require you to create an account or provide any personal credentials. We do not collect, store, or sell:

  • Names, email addresses, or phone numbers (unless you voluntarily reach out for customer support).
  • Biometric data or facial geometry identifiers.
  • GPS location data, IP addresses, or browsing history.

3. Device Permissions & Why They Are Needed

  • Photos (Photo Library): Used solely to load photos you choose to edit and save your exported creations back to your camera roll.
  • Camera: Used solely if you choose to capture a live photo directly within the app for editing. Camera frames are processed in-memory and are never recorded or sent anywhere.

4. Advertising & Rewarded Access

The free version of Photo Inhanser Pro may display optional rewarded video advertisements prior to photo export provided by trusted networks such as Google AdMob.

  • All ad integrations comply strictly with Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) and SKAdNetwork privacy frameworks.
  • Purchasing a Premium subscription removes all advertisements.
  • For offline access without internet connection, an anonymous device identifier is stored in your device's local secure Keychain solely to track your complimentary 3 free offline exports. This counter remains strictly on your device and is never shared.

5. Subscriptions & Payments

Premium subscriptions and lifetime purchases are securely handled by Apple through StoreKit. We never receive, process, or store your credit card or financial information.

6. Analytics & Crash Diagnostics

To ensure high app stability, render performance, and bug tracking, Photo Inhanser Pro may utilize Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. These services collect anonymous performance diagnostics and crash logs that cannot be linked to your identity.

7. Third-Party Services

Photo Inhanser Pro integrates with verified industry-standard partners:

  • Apple App Store (StoreKit)
  • Google AdMob (Mobile Ads)
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics

Each third-party service operates under its respective privacy policies.

8. Children's Privacy

Photo Inhanser Pro does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected from any user, the application is safe for users of all ages.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
