Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Are my photos safe? Do they get uploaded to any server?

Never. Photo Inhanser Pro is engineered with 100% on-device AI architecture. All image editing, AI object erasing, and segmentation are performed locally on your iPhone using Apple Metal and Apple Vision frameworks. Your photos never leave your device.

How does the Free Tier and Offline Mode work?

Online exports are supported by non-intrusive rewarded advertisements. When operating offline without internet access, each device is granted a complimentary quota of 3 free exports. After reaching this limit, connecting to the internet to load an ad or a Premium subscription is required.

How do I restore my past purchases or subscription?

If you reinstalled the app or switched devices, open Settings within the app and tap Restore Purchases. Apple will verify your Apple ID and unlock your active purchases instantly at no extra cost.

How do I cancel or manage an auto-renewable subscription?

All subscriptions are billed securely through Apple. You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime by opening your iPhone’s Settings > [Your Apple ID] > Subscriptions.

Why did an AI Object Erase not remove the entire object?

Ensure you brush completely over the object including any soft shadows it casts. For best results, use a brush stroke slightly wider than the object itself so the background texture can blend seamlessly.
